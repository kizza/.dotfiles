# Further Test

Read the house rules first: `programs/agentic/skills/diagnose/references/briefs/shared.md`
Then read the brief for your angle in this directory — `imaging.md`, `pathology.md`, `vitals.md`, or
`chart-review.md`. It tells you your results file, your id prefix, and whether you owe a specimen label.

Rounds after the initial workup **see the differential**, which the initial workup does not.

Read it as well: `{case_dir}/differential.md`

Your job this round is exactly the one task you were given, and nothing else. It will be phrased as a
task, not a topic — "rule out Dx1: determine whether the guard at `foo.rb:112` is reachable for this
patient given their flags and policies", or "Dx2 is only user error if intent is documented — find where
anyone decided this, or establish that nobody did".

The differential names what each candidate needs. Aim at your task; if you happen to rule out or complete
a DIFFERENT candidate, report that too rather than ignoring it.

**Append** your new results to your results file, continuing the id sequence — do not overwrite what is
already there. Return an impression covering only the new results.

If your task turns out to be unanswerable with the access you have, say so in one line and stop. A round
that reports "this cannot be checked from here, and here is what would be needed" closes the case
honestly; one that substitutes a plausible answer does not.
