# Spoken English

How to word anything that goes through `piper_say`. Keiran hears each sentence once and cannot scroll
back, so every sentence must make sense on the first hearing.

These rules adapt ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English for a listener instead of a reader, by way of
[danyuchn/asd-ste100-skill](https://github.com/danyuchn/asd-ste100-skill) (MIT).

## Shape each sentence

- **One idea a sentence.** Split anything joined by "and then", "which", a semicolon or a dash.
- **Twenty words at most.** Most sentences need fewer.
- **Answer first, then the reason.** "Yes, it's safe. The guard runs first."
- **Active voice, with the actor named.** "The job retries the claim", not "the claim is retried".
- **Simple tenses.** "The spec failed", not "the spec has been failing". Keep a compound tense only
  when it says something the simple one cannot: "it has finished, so the output is there now".
- **Three nouns in a stack at most.** "The handler that sets queue priority", not "the queue priority
  handler class".
- **Keep the small words.** Do not drop a subject, verb or article to save time. "Lines that nobody
  claimed stay open", not "unclaimed lines open".

## Choose plain words

- **One verb, not a phrasal verb.** Start, not kick off. Check, not dig into. Remove, not take out.
- **A verb, not a noun made from one.** "It checks the total", not "it performs a check of the total".
- **One name for one thing.** Once it is "the invoice line", do not switch to "the row" or "the item".
- **No filler and no praise.** No "great question", "it's worth noting", "essentially", "robust".

## Keep the confidence you have

- **A hedge is content.** "It may have failed" stays "may". Do not make a guess sound like a fact to
  shorten it, and do not add a cause you did not find.
- **Say what you have not checked.** "I haven't looked at the spec yet" is a full answer.

## Make it hearable

- **No symbols, markup or code.** Say what the code does and where it is: "line seventeen calls the
  policy".
- **Say names as words.** "The claim builder", not "Finance colon colon SAH colon colon ClaimBuilder".
- **Count a list before you give it.** "Three things. First, ..." Then one sentence for each item.
- **Stop when the point is clear.** The goal is to be understood on one hearing, not to be short.
